# Abuse policy

QasidRelay exists for authorized transactional SMS. Examples include account
verification, password recovery, operational alerts, and private-pilot
notifications sent to users who expect them.

The project does not support:

- unsolicited bulk messaging;
- spam;
- SIM farms;
- OTP interception;
- phishing;
- bypassing carrier, legal, or platform restrictions;
- scraping or importing recipient lists for marketing.

Maintainers may close issues, pull requests, or discussions that request these
uses.

Operators are responsible for carrier terms, local law, recipient consent,
message content, and rate limits.
